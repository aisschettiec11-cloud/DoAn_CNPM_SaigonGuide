Ben Thanh Market is a well-known market in central Ho Chi Minh City. Its stalls offer food, everyday goods, souvenirs and many other products. The southern entrance faces the Le Loi Street and Quach Thi Trang Square area. Visiting the market is an opportunity to observe trading activity and a lively part of city life. Confirm prices, currency and purchase conditions before paying, and take care of personal belongings in crowded areas. Opening arrangements and access routes can change, so check information at the venue.
