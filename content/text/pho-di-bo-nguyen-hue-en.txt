Nguyen Hue Boulevard is a prominent avenue in central Ho Chi Minh City. It stretches from Le Thanh Ton Street near City Hall towards Ton Duc Thang Street and the Saigon River waterfront. Its central pedestrian space was completed in 2015. People come here to stroll, meet friends and enjoy the urban setting. Take care at road crossings, follow event instructions and help keep the public space clean. The map marker represents one reference point along the boulevard, rather than the entire pedestrian area.
