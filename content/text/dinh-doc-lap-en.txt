Independence Palace is a historic landmark in central Ho Chi Minh City. The present building was designed by Vietnamese architect Ngo Viet Thu. It served as the home and workplace of the president of South Vietnam and is closely associated with the events of 30 April 1975. Today it is also known as Reunification Hall and welcomes visitors as well as official events. Take time to observe its architecture and green surroundings. Admission arrangements and accessible rooms may change, so please consult the official venue before your visit.
