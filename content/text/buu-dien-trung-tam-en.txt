Saigon Central Post Office stands near Notre-Dame Cathedral in the Paris Commune Square area. Built in the late nineteenth century, it remains associated with postal services while also welcoming visitors interested in its architecture. Historic maps and decorative details can be seen inside. The building is often incorrectly attributed to Gustave Eiffel; historical references cited by the source identify architect Alfred Foulhoux. Please respect the working areas, avoid obstructing customers and follow staff guidance.
