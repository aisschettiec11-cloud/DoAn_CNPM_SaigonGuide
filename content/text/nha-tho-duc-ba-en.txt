Saigon Notre-Dame Cathedral is a Catholic cathedral in the city centre, close to the Central Post Office. Built between 1877 and 1880, it is recognisable by its red-brick exterior and twin bell towers. Its forecourt offers a place to pause and observe the surrounding city. This is an active place of worship: please dress respectfully, keep noise low and follow local instructions. Restoration work or religious services may affect access. This guide does not guarantee entry to the interior.
