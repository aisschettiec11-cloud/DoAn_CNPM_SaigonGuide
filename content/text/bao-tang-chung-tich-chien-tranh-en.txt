The War Remnants Museum is at 28 Vo Van Tan Street in Ho Chi Minh City. Its collections include documents and objects related to wars in Vietnam, including the First Indochina War and the Vietnam War. The displays invite visitors to consider the effects of conflict on people and society. Some images can be distressing and may not be suitable for young children. Take time to read the captions, approach the material respectfully and follow the museum rules on photography and access.
